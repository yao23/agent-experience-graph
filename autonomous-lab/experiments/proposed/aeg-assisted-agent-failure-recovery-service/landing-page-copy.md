# Unpublished landing-page copy draft

Draft only—do not publish in Phase 0.

## Headline

Turn a reproducible agent or CI failure into an evidence-backed recovery plan.

## Draft body

The proposed AEG-assisted Agent Failure Recovery Service is designed for
independent developers and small teams facing repeated Playwright, CI, or
coding-agent failures. A future qualified engagement would document the
failure, identify the root cause, recommend the smallest supported recovery,
run objective verification, and state limitations plainly.

AEG may surface relevant verified experience or abstain. We would report which
happened and whether it materially changed the work. No success, speed, cost,
or repair-quality improvement is promised.
