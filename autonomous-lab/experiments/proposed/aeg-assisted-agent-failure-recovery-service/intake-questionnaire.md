# Intake questionnaire template

Draft only; do not send or collect responses during Phase 0.

1. What observable command or action reproduces the failure?
2. What exact result should occur, and what happens instead?
3. Which repository revision, runtime, operating system, and dependency versions
   are required?
4. What objective test or oracle determines success?
5. Which retries or repair attempts have already failed?
6. May the supplied material lawfully be shared for this diagnostic?
7. Does it contain employer-owned, proprietary, personal, regulated, or
   security-sensitive information?
8. Can the reproduction be reduced to public or explicitly authorized files?
9. Are credentials, production access, or private network resources required?
10. Is there already a public repair, active PR, fork PR, linked commit, or
    maintainer-owned contribution path?
11. May sanitized, non-identifying lessons be proposed later for reuse? A “yes”
    does not itself authorize publication or promotion.
12. What output is requested: diagnosis only, verified repair, or recovery plan?

Never request passwords, tokens, private keys, personal identifiers, raw private
conversations, or unnecessary full logs. Unsuitable intake is rejected rather
than retained.
