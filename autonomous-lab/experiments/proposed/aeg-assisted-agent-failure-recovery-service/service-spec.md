# AEG-assisted Agent Failure Recovery Service — Phase 0 specification

Status: unpublished repository-local draft. Phase 0 prepares a reviewable
service package; it does not deliver a service, contact a user, run a repair,
publish an offer, or establish demand.

## Intended service

For a qualified reproducible Playwright, CI, or coding-agent failure, a future
delivery would produce:

1. a reproducible failure statement;
2. evidence-backed root-cause analysis;
3. a minimal repair or recovery recommendation;
4. objective verification results;
5. a sanitized reusable experience capsule only when justified;
6. an honest limitations statement; and
7. a customer-facing diagnostic report.

AEG may recommend verified experience or abstain. Ordinary coding-agent work
does the diagnosis and implementation; Autonomous Lab controls state, budgets,
evidence, and approvals. AEG retrieval effect, verified repair evidence,
external acceptance, and commercial demand are separate measurements.

## Evidence boundary

Batch 01 supports orchestration feasibility, objective local checks, calibrated
abstention, and one synthetic mechanism-transfer result. Batch 02 corrected its
only selected task to an independent local reproduction invalid for fresh-task
qualification. Neither batch establishes that AEG improves success rate, cost,
latency, or repair quality; neither establishes product-market fit.

Phase 0 may be reviewed internally. External validation, recruitment, real
repair work, publication, pricing, payment, and Phase 1 require new approval.
