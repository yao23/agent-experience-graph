# Target customer hypothesis

Status: hypothesis for later validation; no users were contacted.

## Initial segment

- Independent developers and small engineering teams.
- Teams using Playwright, CI systems, or coding agents.
- Teams facing a reproducible failure, repeated ineffective retries, or a
  difficult-to-diagnose regression.
- Teams able to provide a lawful, sanitized reproduction and objective oracle.

## Likely trigger

The user has already spent time retrying or narrowing a failure and wants a
bounded diagnosis with explicit evidence and limitations—not an open-ended
consulting engagement or an unsupported promise of repair.

## Exclusions

Reject incidents containing employer-confidential code without authority,
personal data, credentials, regulated data, malware, ambiguous ownership,
production-only access, or no reproducible oracle. See the
[eligibility checklist](eligibility-checklist.md) and
[privacy boundaries](privacy-and-ip-boundaries.md).

No segment, pain intensity, repeat usage, or willingness-to-pay claim has been
externally validated.
