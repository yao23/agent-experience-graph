# Pricing hypotheses

Unpublished hypotheses only. Phase 0 authorizes no offer, price publication,
payment, or customer contact.
No payment was requested, offered, accepted, or processed.

| Hypothesis | Possible range | Includes | Excludes |
|---|---:|---|---|
| First qualified pilots | Free for 3–5 users | bounded intake, diagnosis, evidence report | guaranteed repair, production access, publication |
| Diagnostic only | $49–$99 | reproduction review, root-cause evidence, recommendation | implementation and external contribution |
| Verified repair | $99–$199 | bounded implementation plus frozen verification | broad refactor, deployment, support retainer |

The `$49–$199` range is a testable hypothesis, not a final price.

## No-charge/refund hypothesis

Charge nothing when intake is ineligible, no useful diagnosis is produced, the
oracle cannot be reproduced, ownership/privacy is unclear, or the service stops
before an evidence-backed recommendation. A future paid pilot would need an
approved refund policy before accepting payment.

## Unit-economics fields to measure later

- Founder qualification, diagnosis, verification, reporting, and follow-up
  minutes.
- Model calls, input/output tokens, and dollar cost when exposed; record
  unavailable telemetry as unavailable, never as estimated zero.
- Tooling cost, payment fees, rework, and no-charge rate.
- Gross-margin hypothesis by diagnostic-only and verified-repair delivery.

Move from free to paid only after 3–5 qualified pilots, at least one useful
externally accepted delivery, bounded founder time/model cost, approved payment
and refund terms, and explicit paid-service authorization.
