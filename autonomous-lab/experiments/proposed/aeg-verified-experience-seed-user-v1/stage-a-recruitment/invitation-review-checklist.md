# Individual invitation-review checklist

Complete once for every initial invitation and follow-up before sending it.

- [ ] Recruitment authorization is effective on `main` and no stopping condition is open.
- [ ] Candidate was found through relevant public work or the founder's existing network; no scraping or bulk list was used.
- [ ] Invitation is personalized to the candidate's clearly relevant public work or known coding-agent use.
- [ ] The reviewed protocol template is used and contains no unsupported performance claim.
- [ ] Invitation states this is early research, AEG may abstain or provide no benefit, participation is voluntary and unpaid, and withdrawal is allowed.
- [ ] Invitation prohibits credentials, private repositories, employer-confidential code, customer data, proprietary logs, and raw private conversations.
- [ ] Invitation explains that anonymized evidence retention and public-result publication require separate optional consent.
- [ ] Candidate has received no prior initial invitation, or this is the single allowed follow-up after one initial invitation.
- [ ] No task execution, diagnosis, repair, AEG query, contribution, publication, or payment is promised.
- [ ] Reviewer assigned a pseudonymous candidate ID and review ID before sending.

One completed checklist authorizes only its corresponding message. Nonresponse
does not authorize a follow-up beyond the single allowed follow-up and never
constitutes consent.
