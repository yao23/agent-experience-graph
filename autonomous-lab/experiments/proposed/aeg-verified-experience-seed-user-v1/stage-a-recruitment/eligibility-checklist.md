# Stage A recruitment eligibility checklist

Complete only after the activation record is merged to `main`. Use a
pseudonymous candidate ID. Do not inspect or retain submitted source code,
private repositories, credentials, employer-confidential material, customer
data, proprietary logs, or raw private conversations.

- [ ] Candidate actively uses Codex, Claude Code, Cursor, Copilot, or a similar coding agent.
- [ ] Candidate has one real, current software task.
- [ ] Repository is public, or owner authorization is confirmed by attestation without accessing a private repository.
- [ ] Task has an objective reproducible oracle.
- [ ] No correct public repair is already known during initial screening.
- [ ] Candidate agrees not to submit credentials, proprietary employer code, customer data, or sensitive logs.
- [ ] Participation is voluntary and unpaid; nonresponse is not consent.
- [ ] Eligibility-record retention consent is recorded separately.
- [ ] Optional anonymized experimental-evidence retention consent remains separate and is not requested as a condition of recruitment.
- [ ] Optional public-result publication consent remains separate and is not requested as a condition of recruitment.
- [ ] Candidate has received no promise of AEG benefit, task repair, contribution, publication, or continued participation.

Passing this checklist authorizes eligibility only. It does not authorize task
execution, AEG retrieval, diagnosis, repair, or external-project activity.
