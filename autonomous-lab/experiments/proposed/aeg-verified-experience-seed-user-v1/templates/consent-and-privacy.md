# DRAFT — DO NOT SEND: participant consent and privacy

Before any task evidence is retained, the participant must affirm each item:

- I am voluntarily participating and may stop or withdraw retained evidence.
- I understand this is an early research experiment, AEG may abstain or provide
  no benefit, and no payment is offered.
- I am authorized to use the submitted repository and task for this experiment.
- I understand AEG may recommend a relevant experience, abstain, or provide an
  irrelevant or misleading result; no repair or contribution is promised.
- I consent to retaining only sanitized commands, tests, UTC timestamps,
  retrieval scores, agent actions, objective results, and my 1–5 usefulness
  rating under pseudonymous identifiers.
- I will not submit credentials, private repositories, employer-confidential
  code, customer data, proprietary logs, raw private conversations, private
  absolute paths, personal data, or material I lack permission to use.
- I understand evidence retention consent does not grant publication consent.
  Publishing a repository name, quote, patch, task detail, or result requires a
  separate explicit choice after I review the proposed material.

Required record: participant ID, consent version, UTC consent time, retention
choice, publication choice (`not_requested`, `declined`, or `approved`), and any
withdrawal/deletion time. Do not store the participant's name, handle, email, or
raw consent conversation in the repository.
