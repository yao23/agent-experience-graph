## Material finding
Three high-information patterns emerged:

1. **Commerce totals can remain mutable after checkout.** Instacart documents that substitutions/refunds can change service fees and the final cost appears on the post-order receipt.
2. **Fee policies can be effective-dated and entitlement-conditioned.** Southwest’s current checked-bag fees depend on booking/change date, fare, loyalty/card status, operating carrier, and bag characteristics.
3. **A displayed “total” can still have collection-scope exclusions.** Airbnb’s U.S. total price is fee-inclusive before taxes, while taxes Airbnb does not collect may remain outside the displayed amount.

H-COMMERCE should therefore test whether verified Experience helps agents preserve value finality, policy windows, entitlement context, and collection responsibility rather than memorizing a number.
