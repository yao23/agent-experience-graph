## Material finding

This scan found a new H-COMMERCE failure mode beyond the previous `variant/seller/time` and `stable-vs-dynamic` findings: **consumer agents also need evidence-precedence and transaction-eligibility rules across multiple public surfaces**.

Two concrete patterns appeared:

1. **Same retailer, conflicting public surfaces** — a comparison/search surface can expose a price or product attribute that a canonical product page or official policy scopes differently.
2. **Displayed commercial fact != actionable commercial entitlement** — seeing a price does not prove a user/agent is authorized to buy at that price, and seeing a retailer specification does not prove that specification is authoritative when a manufacturer source conflicts.

A reusable commerce Experience may therefore need to encode not only `what fact was observed`, but also **which surface/source had precedence, what entitlement/context was required, and what to do when sources conflict**.

## Cross-candidate schema/protocol implications

New **candidate-only / proposed** commerce context fields suggested by this scan:

- `source_surface`: e.g. `CANONICAL_PRODUCT | SEARCH_INDEX | COMPARISON | HELP_POLICY | MANUFACTURER_SPEC`
- `source_authority`: e.g. `MANUFACTURER | RETAILER_CANONICAL | RETAILER_AUXILIARY | THIRD_PARTY_INDEX`
- `entitlement_scope`: e.g. `PUBLIC | MEMBER | ACCOUNT_REQUIRED | LOCATION_REQUIRED | UNKNOWN`
- `policy_precedence`: explicit item-level vs generic policy resolution
- `conflict_state`: `NONE | CONFLICTING_CLAIMS | NEEDS_REVALIDATION | UNRESOLVED`
- `claim_resolution`: provenance-preserving record of how conflicting claims were handled
- existing/proposed `commercial_fact_scope`, `freshness_class`, `observed_at`, `locality_scope`, `claim_origin`, `evidence_kind`

All remain `PROPOSED_NOT_VALIDATED`. Do not use them for Registry ranking or a synthetic accessibility score without paired evidence.
