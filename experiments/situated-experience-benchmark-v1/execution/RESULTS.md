## Mechanical decision

Promotion is not supported and fails closed. No correctness comparison or
interpretable repair-path improvement exists, treatment token overhead was not
measured, and end-to-end leakage freedom was not demonstrated on a real
substrate. No frozen benchmark stop condition was triggered by an observed arm
outcome because no arm ran; instead, the prerequisite substrate gate blocked
execution.

Do not expand S1 to full historical dependency stacks yet. First provision and
independently canary a disposable runner plus credential broker, private raw
output retention, sanitized return channel, and token telemetry for both modes.
